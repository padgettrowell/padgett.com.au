Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 10 Feb, 2025


Coach Log values your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.


1. Information Collection and Use


We do not collect, store, or share any personal information on external servers. However, Coach Log uses iCloud to synchronise your app data across your own devices.


• iCloud Synchronisation: The data stored in iCloud is private and accessible only to you and your iCloud-connected devices. We do not have access to this data.

• The data stored in iCloud is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.


2. No Third-Party Services


Our app does not use any third-party services that collect user data beyond Apple’s iCloud services for synchronization.


3. Changes to This Privacy Policy


We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our app’s functionality or legal requirements. We will notify users by updating the “Effective Date” at the top of this policy.


4. Contact Us


If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us at coachlog@padgett.com.au
